# Back Matter

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1) Can I do this if I still lift weights?
Yes. Use this programme as a 3-day primary block, or run two isometric sessions and one lighter dynamic session per week. Keep total stress realistic.

### 2) Is this only for injured men?
No. It is for men who want durable strength and lower joint cost. You can be pain-free and still benefit.

### 3) How hard should each hold feel?
Most work should sit around 6–8/10 effort. Short overcoming efforts can rise to 8–9/10 once breathing and position are stable.

### 4) What if I miss a week?
Resume at the previous week’s prescription for 2–3 sessions before progressing.

### 5) How long should I stay with isometric training?
As long as it delivers results. Many men keep isometrics as a permanent base and layer dynamic work around them.

## Quick Start (One-Page Summary)

- Train 3 non-consecutive days per week.
- Keep sessions to ~30 minutes.
- Follow block order: Activation -> Structural Strength -> Joint Armour -> Log.
- Build hold time before chasing maximal effort.
- Breathe continuously; do not strain with held breath.
- Track next-day joint response.
- Progress only when posture, breathing, and recovery are stable.

---

## Minimum Effective Week (Busy Schedule Version)

If life is chaotic, do this instead of skipping training:

- 2 sessions (20–25 minutes)
- 3 structural holds per session
- 1 overcoming drill per session
- 1 finisher drill per session

Even reduced training preserves rhythm and tissue tolerance.
