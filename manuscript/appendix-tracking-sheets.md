## 3. Progression Decision Guide

Progress only if at least three of the following are true:

- You completed all sets with stable posture.
- Breathing stayed controlled (no breath-holding strain).
- Joint discomfort remained mild and settled quickly.
- Confidence under tension improved.

If two or fewer are true, keep the same prescription next session.

If warning pain occurs, reduce duration, effort, or leverage immediately.

---

## 4. Pain-Aware Scaling Card

### Productive discomfort (usually acceptable)

- Local muscular burn
- General effort discomfort
- Mild stiffness after training that settles by next day

### Warning signs (stop or scale)

- Sharp, catching, or unstable joint pain
- Pain that worsens during a hold
- Symptoms that alter normal movement pattern
- Persistent increase in pain beyond 48 hours

When warning signs appear:

1. Stop that drill.
2. Switch to easier leverage or shorter duration.
3. Reduce intensity by 1–2 effort points.
4. Reassess in the next session.
