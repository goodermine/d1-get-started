# Exercise Library – Isometric Strength Blueprint

This library provides practical setup, coaching cues, regressions, and progressions for the core drills.

---

## 1. Wall March Hold

**Purpose:** hip stability, trunk stiffness, gait pattern control.

**Setup**
1. Stand facing a wall, hands lightly on wall at shoulder height.
2. Step one foot back slightly and lift opposite knee to hip height.
3. Keep ribs stacked over pelvis.

**Hold:** 10–30 seconds each side.

**Key cues**
- Drive standing foot into floor.
- Keep lifted foot relaxed.
- Avoid arching lower back.

**Regression:** lower the knee height.  
**Progression:** remove hand support intermittently.

---

## 2. Incline Push-Up Mid Hold

**Purpose:** anterior chain strength with shoulder-friendly loading.

**Setup**
1. Place hands on wall, bench-height edge, or sturdy support.
2. Lower into mid-range push-up position.
3. Hold with elbows at roughly 45 degrees.

**Hold:** 20–40 seconds.

**Regression:** increase hand height (more upright body).  
**Progression:** lower hand height or extend hold duration.

---

## 3. Split Squat Hover Hold

**Purpose:** knee and hip stability under unilateral load.

**Setup**
1. Take a split stance.
2. Lower until both knees are comfortably bent.
3. Hover above bottom position, torso upright.

**Hold:** 15–35 seconds each side.

**Regression:** shorten range and use fingertip support on wall.  
**Progression:** deeper hover or longer holds.

---

## 4. Doorframe Chest Press Overcoming

**Purpose:** high-intent pressing without external load.

**Setup**
1. Stand in split stance facing doorframe.
2. Place palms on frame at chest height.
3. Brace body and press into frame.

**Effort:** 6–10 seconds.

**Cues**
- Ramp force gradually.
- Exhale lightly during effort.
- Keep neck relaxed.

**Regression:** reduce intent to 6–7/10.  
**Progression:** 8–9/10 intent with strict breathing.

---

## 5. Doorframe Row Isometric / Overcoming Pull

**Purpose:** upper back, grip, and shoulder stabilisation.

**Setup**
1. Grip both sides of a sturdy doorframe.
2. Lean body back slightly.
3. Pull chest towards frame without movement.

**Hold:** 20–40 sec (structural) or 6–10 sec (overcoming).

**Regression:** stand more upright.  
**Progression:** lean further back with firmer brace.

---

## 6. Hip Hinge Wall Reach Hold

**Purpose:** posterior chain tension and lumbar-friendly hinge mechanics.

**Setup**
1. Stand 15–20 cm from wall, facing away.
2. Push hips back towards wall while keeping spine long.
3. Reach hands forward to counterbalance.

**Hold:** 20–35 seconds.

**Regression:** reduce hinge depth.  
**Progression:** single-leg kickstand stance.

---

## 7. Glute Bridge Hold

**Purpose:** glute and hamstring support for pelvis and low back.

**Setup**
1. Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat.
2. Lift hips until trunk and thighs align.
3. Hold without over-arching.

**Hold:** 20–40 seconds.

**Regression:** shorter hold with lower hip height.  
**Progression:** single-leg bridge hold 10–20 sec each side.

---

## 8. Wall Sit / Wall Squat Hover

**Purpose:** knee extensor endurance and tendon tolerance.

**Setup**
1. Back against wall, feet slightly forward.
2. Slide down to tolerated depth.
3. Keep weight distributed through mid-foot.

**Hold:** 20–40 seconds.

**Regression:** shallower angle.  
**Progression:** longer hold or one-leg emphasis (light).

---

## 9. Side Plank

**Purpose:** lateral core stability and shoulder integration.

**Setup**
1. Elbow under shoulder.
2. Lift hips and form straight line head-to-feet (or knees).

**Hold:** 15–30 sec each side.

**Regression:** bent knees.  
**Progression:** top leg raise for brief intervals.

---

## 10. Dead Bug Press Hold

**Purpose:** trunk stiffness with limb dissociation.

**Setup**
1. Lie on back, hips and knees at 90 degrees.
2. Press opposite hand into opposite thigh.
3. Maintain neutral lower back.

**Hold:** 15–30 sec each side.

**Regression:** keep both feet on floor.  
**Progression:** extend non-working leg slightly.

---

## 11. Chin Tuck Wall Hold

**Purpose:** neck endurance and postural control.

**Setup**
1. Stand against wall, back of head lightly touching.
2. Tuck chin straight back (not down).
3. Hold gentle tension.

**Hold:** 15–25 sec.

**Regression:** seated version away from wall.  
**Progression:** add light arm position challenge.

---

## 12. Forearm Isometric Series (Self-Resistance)

**Purpose:** elbow/forearm resilience for gripping and pulling tasks.

**Drills**
- Wrist flexion isometric
- Wrist extension isometric
- Pronation hold
- Supination hold

**Prescription:** 2–3 sets of 15–25 sec each direction.

**Intensity:** begin at 6/10 and build carefully.
