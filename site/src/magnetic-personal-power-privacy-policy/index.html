Public Policy Page

Privacy Policy

Magnetic Personal Power is an Android app created by Aaron Ellis. The app provides offline hypnosis, relaxation, and personal development audio, along with embedded support booklets.

App: Magnetic Personal Power
Developer: Aaron Ellis
Effective date: 2 June 2026

1. Introduction

Magnetic Personal Power is designed to work offline on the user’s device.

The app provides hypnosis, relaxation, and personal development audio sessions, together with embedded support booklets bundled inside the app package.

The app is intended to be straightforward: no account, no backend, no tracking, and no internet requirement for normal use.

2. Information We Collect

Magnetic Personal Power does not collect personal information from users.

  • No account or login is required.
  • No names, email addresses, location data, or personal identifiers are collected.
  • No health data, payment information, contacts, photos, camera data, or microphone recordings are collected.
  • No analytics, ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, cloud sync, or backend data collection are used.

3. How The App Uses Information

Because the app does not collect personal information, no personal information is used, sold, shared, transmitted, or processed by Aaron Ellis through the app.

Playback happens locally on the user’s device using audio and booklet files bundled inside the app.

4. Local Device Storage

The app may store limited information locally on the user’s device, such as whether the intro video should be shown again.

If a user chooses to download an embedded PDF booklet, the file is saved locally to the user’s device.

These local preferences or files remain on the device and are not sent to Aaron Ellis or any external server.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, cloud syncing, social login, or third-party tracking tools.

The app does not require an internet connection for normal playback and reading functions.

6. Data Sharing

Magnetic Personal Power does not sell, rent, trade, or share user personal information.

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal data transmission or distribution by the app.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect information from children.

Since the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Health / Wellness Disclaimer

The app provides relaxation and personal development audio only. It is not medical treatment and is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or mental health care.

Users should not listen while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that requires full attention.

Plain-language summary

Magnetic Personal Power works locally on the device. It does not ask for an account, does not track the user, does not transmit personal data, and does not rely on a backend server.

9. Changes To This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
